William Tell score data (src/scores/tell.mjs):
Gioachino Rossini; Midica transcription by Jan Trukenmüller.
Mozilla Public License 2.0: see MPL-2.0.txt.
Source: https://github.com/truj/midica/blob/master/examples/rossini_william_tell_overture_finale-lowlevel.midica
Adaptation: full polyphonic finale parsed into millisecond note tuples; 22 historically verified flute notes corrected down an octave; lead targets selected for playability. The adapted score data remains MPL-2.0.
Original editable notation is included as william-tell-source.midica and william-tell-highlevel.midica; exact octave changes are included as william-tell-corrections.json. The adapted editable source is available in this repository at src/scores/tell.mjs:
