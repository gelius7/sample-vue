## Source progression and chart verification

Sources and terms were checked on 2026-10-05. The current Incompetech track-page code loads the official `pieces.json` catalog and displays a CC BY 4.0 attribution notice for each selected title. The catalog confirms the three titles, ISRC identifiers, MP3 filenames, and listed tempos of 120, 115, and 128 BPM. The official licensing page is https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/ .

The source-beginning corrections were made on 2026-10-05 from the previously obtained official MP3s. Every cut is sample-aligned. Electrodoodle is byte-for-byte unchanged. Disco Medusae now includes its first 41.739 seconds and complete original ending. EDM Detection Mode now includes its first 30 seconds, preserves the actual sequence of sections through 240 seconds, and is labeled “처음부터 4분 발췌” in the selector. Its 238.125–240 second transition is measurably quieter than the preceding phrase, and the edit stops before the next section's full beat. Musical section labels and boundary selection are based on source waveform/rhythm analysis, not a claimed listening review.

Targets are selected musical accents on the sixteenth-note grid, with section-specific, authored lane gestures. A recurring source hook recalls the same gesture; the entire game is not a repeated copy of one chart. Other accompaniment is deliberately omitted, and quiet passages are not filled with artificial targets. These charts are not automatic pitch estimates or full note-for-note transcriptions.
